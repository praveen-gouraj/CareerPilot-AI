Project Overview

One platform for the complete placement journey

Instead of using separate tools for career recommendations, skill analysis, resume review, and interview preparation, this system brings the complete workflow into one platform.

Discover

Identify suitable career domains based on your skills, interests, and current capabilities.

Analyze

Compare your existing skills with target career requirements and identify areas that need improvement.

Improve

Analyze your resume using NLP-based keyword matching and receive practical suggestions for improvement.

Prepare

Practice technical and HR interviews while monitoring your overall placement preparation progress.

The Challenge

The problem students face

Students often struggle to determine which career path matches their current skills. At the same time, identifying missing skills, improving resumes, preparing for interviews, and understanding placement readiness can require multiple disconnected resources.

Our Objective

Make career preparation more structured

The objective of this project is to provide students with an intelligent, centralized platform that converts their skills and preparation data into actionable career guidance, learning recommendations, resume insights, and interview preparation support.

Intelligent Technology

Powered by AI, ML and NLP

The system combines machine learning, natural language processing, analytics, and secure web technologies to provide an integrated career guidance experience.

Machine Learning

K-Nearest Neighbors

KNN analyzes the selected skill profile and recommends career domains that closely match the student's capabilities.

Natural Language Processing

NLP Resume Analysis

Resume text is extracted and analyzed for relevant keywords to generate ATS-style insights and recommendations.

Data Visualization

Progress Analytics

Interactive charts help students understand their performance and track improvement over time.

Web Platform

Secure Flask Application

Authentication, sessions, role-based access, database management, and secure application workflows are handled through the Flask backend.
